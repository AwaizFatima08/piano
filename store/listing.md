## Full description
Rainbow Keys is a first piano for toddlers and little kids. Every key glows, a string of party bulbs ripples across the screen, and stars, hearts and music notes fly up with every note.

FIVE WAYS TO PLAY
• Piano: piano, xylophone, music box or space sounds
• Drums: boom, clap, crash and ding
• Silly sounds: boing, pop, quack, zap and more
• Magic songs: any key plays the next note of Twinkle Twinkle, Mary Had a Little Lamb, Row Your Boat, Old MacDonald, Hot Cross Buns or London Bridge, or tap "Play for me"
• Find it: one key shines; find it to earn a star, and five stars bring a confetti party

MADE FOR LITTLE HANDS
• Big, bright keys with friendly animals; play with several fingers at once or slide across the keys
• 5 big keys or 8 keys
• Animals, letters, Do Re Mi or colours on the keys

GROWN-UPS IN CHARGE
• Volume slider, plus a "loudest volume allowed" limit
• Soft glowing lights that never strobe; choose Party, Gentle or Calm
• Hide the mode buttons to keep a child in one mode
• Settings sit behind a parent gate (hold the gear, then answer a sum)

SAFE AND SIMPLE
• No login, no accounts, no chat, no analytics
• All sounds are made by the app
• Free, with one small family-safe ad only on the grown-ups settings screen; never on the play screen
